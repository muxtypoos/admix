# Admix: Local Anaesthetic Calculator

A quick calculator for the maximum safe dose of local anaesthetics, including mixtures, dilutions, airway lidocaine and lipid rescue (LAST) doses. Works on a phone or computer.

**Open it:** https://muxtypoos.github.io/admix/

## Want a version for your organisation?

We can set up your own list of local anaesthetics and maximum doses, your preferred lipid guideline, your wording and your colours. Any improvement we make to Admix reaches your version too.

1. Fill in the request form. It is already filled in with the current defaults, so you only change what you need:
   - **Excel (easiest):** download [Admix-request-form.xlsx](Admix-request-form.xlsx), fill it in and email it to Dr George Lui, george.lui@nhs.net.
   - **On GitHub:** Issues, then New issue, then "Submit your organisation's request form", and drag your completed file into the box. An automatic check reads it and prepares your version for review; if something in the form needs fixing, a reply on your request tells you what.
2. We build it and send you a link for review.
3. Your clinical lead checks and signs off the doses and wording.
4. We send you the final link (and the Android app if you want one).

Use the free text box at the end of the form for anything else you need.

## Important

Admix is decision support only. It does not replace clinical judgement or local policy. Every organisation must check its own doses and guidelines before using it with patients. The version and configuration code at the bottom of the app (for example `Admix v0.3.0-beta · default · ef222e02`) identify exactly which version you are using: please quote it in any feedback.
