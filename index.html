Formulas & references

LBW (kg, H in cm, W in kg, always capped at TBW): Janmahasatian: M 9270W/(6680+216·BMI), F 9270W/(8780+244·BMI). Boer: M 0.407W+0.267H−19.2, F 0.252W+0.473H−48.3. James: M 1.1W−128(W/H)², F 1.07W−148(W/H)². Hume: M 0.3281W+0.33929H−29.5336, F 0.29569W+0.41813H−43.2933. Peters: 3.8×0.0215×W^0.6469×H^0.7236.

Dosing weight: TBW below the BMI threshold; between threshold and threshold+5 a linear blend towards LBW (adjusted weight, as in the source spreadsheet; the strict option switches straight to LBW at the threshold); LBW above (SOBA). Expert option "Hold": above the threshold the weight is held at that of a patient with BMI equal to the threshold and the same height (it never falls, but is above lean weight). Lipid rescue doses use lean body weight in that case.

Paediatric mode (offered if age ≤16 y, height ≤1.2 m or weight <30 kg; entered manually): the BMI threshold is the chosen RCPCH BMI centile (UK-WHO/UK90, 2–20 y, by sex) at the child’s age. Below it TBW is used; from the threshold to 110% of it the dosing weight blends from TBW to LBW; above that LBW is used (Peters by default). Without age (2–20 y), sex or height, TBW is used. Centile lines were digitised from the RCPCH charts.

Lidocaine pool = total mg/kg LBW (no absolute mg cap). Airway lidocaine used reduces it and the remaining fraction scales every agent's dosing weight.

Max volume (ml) = weight × mg/kg ÷ mg/ml. Dual agents: V1/Max1 + V2/Max2 ≤ 1. Dilution: V_high = C_low·V_final/C_high.

LAST lipid emulsion 20% (W = weight in kg), Association of Anaesthetists QRH 3-10 v.2 (2023), Box A: bolus = 1.5 × W ml over 2–3 min and start an infusion of 15 × W ml/h (= 0.25 × W ml/min) immediately. At 5 and 10 min, if cardiovascular stability has not been restored or an adequate circulation deteriorates: repeat the bolus (same dose). At any time after 5 min, if still unstable: double the rate to 30 × W ml/h. Do not exceed the maximum cumulative dose of 12 × W ml. In circulatory arrest use a smaller adrenaline dose (≤1 mcg/kg instead of 1 mg) and avoid vasopressin. (The QRH 70 kg example is rounded to ~100 ml and 17.5 ml/min; the calculator uses exact per-kg figures.) The QRH does not state which weight to use in obesity. Source: Association of Anaesthetists 2023, www.anaesthetists.org/qrh, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

LAST, ASRA 2020 checklist: over 70 kg (strictly over): bolus ~100 ml over 2–3 min, then ~250 ml over 15–20 min (≈750–1000 ml/h). 70 kg or under: bolus ~1.5 × W ml (maximum 100 ml) over 2–3 min, infusion ~0.25 × W ml/min (= 15 × W ml/h; consider a pump below 40 kg). If still unstable: repeat the bolus and double the infusion (30 × W ml/h under 70 kg, ≈1500–2000 ml/h over 70 kg). No fixed repeat limit is stated. Continue lipid for ≥15 min once haemodynamically stable. Maximum lipid dose = 12 × W ml. Epinephrine: start ≤1 mcg/kg.

Weight W for lipid rescue: the dosing weight (TBW, adjusted or LBW, or the manual choice), except lean body weight when the Expert "hold" option has fixed the dosing weight at the BMI threshold weight. It is never the airway-scaled weight. The footnote under the lipid doses states the weight used.

Default limits are commonly quoted reference values within UK practice. Verify against local guidance and edit as required.
